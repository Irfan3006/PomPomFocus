# 🐰 PomPomFocus – Cute & Minimal Pomodoro Timer  

**PomPomFocus** is a simple, cute, and minimal web timer that helps you stay focused, whether you’re studying, working, or just trying to build better habits.  

It’s made for everyone, no tech skills needed. Open it, set your time, and let the bunny keep you on track! 🌸  

👉 **Try it here:** [https://pompomfocus.vercel.app/](https://pompomfocus.vercel.app) 

---

## ✨ Main Features  

### 🎯 Focus & Break Timer  
- Classic Pomodoro: **25 minutes focus**, **5 minutes break**  
- Customize your own durations easily  
- Start, Pause, Reset, or Skip sessions anytime  
- Automatically switch between focus and break  
- See how many sessions you’ve completed  

### 🎨 Minimal & Adorable Design  
- Clean and distraction-free interface  
- Soft pastel colors (with optional **Pink Mode**)  
- Fully responsive, works beautifully on phone, tablet, and laptop  
- Light and Dark mode support  

### 📊 Simple Stats  
- Track total focus time and completed sessions  
- See your daily streak  
- Everything is saved locally, no account needed  

### 🔊 Gentle Sounds  
- Calm notification sounds when the timer ends  
- Adjustable volume and mute option  

### ⚙️ Easy Settings  
- Change focus or break times  
- Turn browser notifications on or off  
- All preferences save automatically
